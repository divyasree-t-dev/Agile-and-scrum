# Day 4 – Set 3: E-Learning Platform – Scope Change Handling

## Overview
This assignment demonstrates how a Scrum Team manages scope changes during an active sprint in an e-learning platform development project.

## Business Scenario
A development team is working on a 10-day sprint with a velocity of 30 story points. On Day 6, the Product Owner requests a certificate download feature for an upcoming launch.

## Tasks Covered
1. Sprint planning and Sprint Goal definition.
2. Analysis of mid-sprint scope changes and their impact.
3. Daily stand-up statements and Scrum Master blocker resolution.
4. Sprint velocity calculation, including partially completed stories.
5. Sprint retrospective using Start-Stop-Continue.
6. Explanation of automation and its role in the Definition of Done.

## Key Outcomes
- Planned sprint capacity: 30 story points.
- New feature: Certificate Download.
- Proposed scope replacement: Course Notifications.
- Completed sprint velocity: 25 story points.
- Retrospective: Three SMART improvement actions.
- Automation: Unit testing, linting, and CI/CD pipeline checks.

## Conclusion
The assignment demonstrates Agile scope management, effective team communication, blocker resolution, velocity tracking, and continuous improvement using Scrum practices.
